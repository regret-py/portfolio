Hero poster. The hero shows assets/img/stage.jpg / .webp until the live render takes over, and keeps it when WebGL2 is missing. It must be the same picture as the live render: render it again here after any change to the scene in assets/js/stage.js.

This renders the whole frame (1920×800) with the light at rest and the light theme's exposure, then saves stage.png. Export it as stage.jpg and stage.webp into assets/img/ (for example with squoosh.app, or cwebp -q 78 stage.png -o stage.webp). Serve the site locally (python3 -m http.server) and open /tools/stage-poster.html.
